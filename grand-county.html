Land Surveying in Grand County, Colorado

Baseline Surveys provides practical, accurate surveying support throughout Grand County.

Grand County features wide valleys, forested parcels, and mountain recreation communities that require accurate site understanding. We perform boundary surveys, topographic mapping, and construction staking for residential and light commercial development. Local projects often depend on reliable terrain data to support grading and access planning. Baseline Surveys provides land surveying in Grand County with practical turnaround for owners and contractors.

Communities We Serve

Granby

Granby development commonly includes larger parcels, cabins, and newer residential lots. We provide land surveying in Granby to support boundary confirmation, design layouts, and construction planning. Clients looking for surveyors near Granby count on our experience with mountain parcels and changing terrain.

Winter Park

Winter Park projects often involve resort-area conditions and elevation-sensitive design. Our land surveying in Winter Park helps define legal boundaries and site features for homes, lodges, and supporting infrastructure. Accurate field control supports smoother permitting and construction execution.

Kremmling

Kremmling sits at the crossroads of several highway corridors and supports ranching, recreation, and rural residential development. We provide land surveying in Kremmling for boundary work, access easements, and site layout on larger parcels. Clients in the area rely on accurate survey control for both infrastructure and property-related projects.
