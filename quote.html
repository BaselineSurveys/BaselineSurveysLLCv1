About Land Surveying in Colorado

Surveying in Colorado often includes additional considerations such as steep or remote terrain, elevation differences, and seasonal access (snow). These factors can affect the time and cost of a survey. Exact pricing depends on property size, site access, deliverables, required research, and overall project scope.

  • Boundary Survey (residential): Depends on lot size, access, terrain, and title/record research needs.
  • Topographic Survey: Depends on acreage, contour detail, and required mapping deliverables.
  • ALTA/NSPS Survey (commercial): Depends on parcel size, title research, and lender requirements.
  • Construction Staking: Depends on project scope, duration, and field coordination needs.
  • LiDAR Scanning & Mapping: Depends on site size, required detail, and deliverable format.
  • Photogrammetry & Drone Mapping: Depends on acreage, imagery requirements, and final output needs.
  • Additional services like as-built mapping, utility locating, and custom aerial deliverables are quoted separately.
