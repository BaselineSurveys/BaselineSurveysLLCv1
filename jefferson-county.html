Land Surveying in Jefferson County, Colorado

Baseline Surveys provides practical, accurate surveying support throughout Jefferson County.

Jefferson County includes foothill communities, suburban neighborhoods, and transportation-linked development areas. We provide boundary surveys, improvement surveys, and construction staking for residential and commercial projects. A reliable survey baseline helps owners and project teams coordinate design, permitting, and build phases. Baseline Surveys provides land surveying in Jefferson County for both mountain-adjacent and urban-edge properties.
