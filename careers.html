About the Survey Crew Role

No surveying experience is required—we provide on-the-job training. Crew members assist with boundary, improvement, ALTA, and topographic surveys, as well as construction layout and staking. Day-to-day work may include setting up and carrying surveying equipment, taking and recording accurate field measurements, locating and documenting property or site features, helping stake utilities, foundations, roads, and grading, and entering or organizing field information on a computer. Applicants should have a high school diploma (or equivalent), basic reading and writing skills, basic math skills, and basic computer literacy.
